Menu Bar Calendar

A personal macOS menu bar plugin for SwiftBar.

Menu Bar Calendar shows upcoming Google Calendar events in the macOS menu bar: the next event with a countdown, and the rest of today's and tomorrow's schedule in a dropdown.

It is a personal tool. It runs only on its owner's Mac and is not offered as a public service.

It requests read-only access to Google Calendar (calendar.readonly) and never creates, changes, or deletes events.
